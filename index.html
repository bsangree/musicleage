How to Play

1
🎵

Submit a Song

Each round has a unique theme. Submit the song you think best fits — it can be anything from any era or genre.

2
🎧

Listen & Vote

Once everyone submits, an anonymous playlist is created. Listen through and vote for your favorites before the deadline.

3
🏆

Earn Points & Win

Votes turn into points. After all rounds are complete, the player with the most points wins the league!

Everything you need for epic music battles

🎯

Themed Rounds

Choose from curated themes or create your own. "Deep Cuts," "Songs from a Movie," "Your Walk-Up Song" — the possibilities are endless.

🔒

Anonymous Submissions

Nobody knows who submitted what until voting closes. Vote on the song, not the person.

💬

Comments & Reactions

Leave comments on tracks as you vote. When results are revealed, see everyone's reactions and spark music conversations.

📊

Leaderboards

Track standings round by round. Every vote counts toward your league total. Can you stay on top?

📱

Play Anywhere

Web, iOS, and Android. Your league is always one tap away whether you're on the couch or on the go.

🎲

Flexible Leagues

Set your own number of rounds, voting window, and invite whoever you want. You control the rules.
